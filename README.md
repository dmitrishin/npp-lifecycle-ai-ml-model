# Formalization of a Nuclear Power Plant Life-Cycle Model for AI and Machine Learning Applications

## Overview

This work presents a formal, research-oriented model designed to support
artificial intelligence and machine learning applications for nuclear power
plant life-cycle management.

The model provides a unified framework for representing NPP structure,
semantics, source documents and datasets, quantitative metrics, and AI/ML
orchestration. It is intended to support research and practical applications
across engineering, construction, commissioning and startup, and
decommissioning.

The model consists of five interoperable components:

- **M — Structure:** a multi-level graph representation of project and plant
  objects, dependencies, and hierarchical classifications;
- **S — Semantics:** an ontological and linguistic domain layer for concepts,
  relations, terminology, semantic consistency, and reasoning;
- **D — Corpus:** typed links between model elements and source documents or
  datasets, supporting traceability and evidence-based validation;
- **Q — Quantitative Layer:** typed attributes and metrics for readiness, risk,
  cost, and other analytical variables and AI/ML features;
- **U — Meta-Level:** an orchestration layer coordinating M, S, D, and Q and,
  in its ML extension, supporting dataset generation, training, evaluation,
  and adaptation.

The framework is modular and does not require full implementation. Individual
components may be adapted, simplified, extended, or implemented selectively
for specific research tasks and practical NPP applications.

The principles and methods of the model may also be transferable to other
complex engineering systems.
